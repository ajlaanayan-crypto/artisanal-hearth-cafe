The Hearth Philosophy

The Roastery

Our journey begins long before the first drop hits the cup. We partner directly with small-lot farmers in high-altitude regions, ensuring fair compensation and sustainable practices.

Every batch is roasted in-house on our vintage cast-iron roaster, carefully monitored by human senses, not just algorithms. We believe the slight imperfections in a manual roast are what give our coffee its soul.
